# Relationships and life's milestones: ecosystem strategy

Updated 29 October 2026 (America/New_York). Private working strategy for founder review.

## Founder direction and decision status

WeddingOS, Jodibana/JodiUSA and Memoire Studio are independent products connected by relationships, celebrations and memories across life's milestones. This direction comes from the founder. The positioning, implementation backlog and experiments below are proposals. Pricing, partner compensation and every campaign remain pending separate founder approval. Nothing in this document authorizes publication, outreach, shared accounts or cross-product personal-data transfer.

Working narrative: **Meet. Celebrate. Remember.** This is proposed campaign language, not a new company name. Customers may enter at any point and use only one product. Dating does not imply marriage, marriage does not imply children, and family circumstances must never be inferred for targeting. Memoire Studio extends the ecosystem beyond weddings to engagements, baby showers, graduations, anniversaries, reunions, travel and other personally chosen milestones.

## Acquisition and retention design

Each product needs its own landing page, promise, activation measure and approval package. A shared ecosystem page can explain the connection after its destinations and claims are validated. The ecosystem is an additional source of qualified introductions, not a substitute for making each product useful on its own.

1. **WeddingOS first:** use the existing free planning tools as the clearest useful public entry point. Target engaged couples with budget/checklist education; separately offer planners a demonstration of workflows actually available. Confirm the registered save flow before describing save limits in promotional copy.
2. **Jodibana:** recruit a small, relevant operator pilot through founder relationships and permissioned community introductions. Demonstrate the current launch wizard and template choices. Measure an operating community, not a generated tenant or an unverified subscription row.
3. **JodiUSA:** concentrate on one founder-selected community/geography with sufficient participation and operational support. Invitation access must have a real owner and response process. Resolve branding, legal placeholders, support and consent questions before promotion. Do not send broad traffic into an invitation form with no clear way to obtain an invitation.
4. **Memoire Studio:** validate the preview, export/order and fulfillment journey, then prepare permissioned examples across weddings and other milestones. Photographers and event partners are a natural channel once deliverables and unit costs are verified. Do not advertise printing, shipping dates or unlimited storage without evidence.

Zero paid advertising means no purchased reach in this plan. Founder time, content production, hosting, support, email and partner servicing still have a cost. Begin with reusable tools and guides, existing permissioned audiences, approved community contributions and partner demonstrations. No bought lists, scraped personal profiles or automated unsolicited outreach.

Initial implementation should use ordinary links and separate signups. Shared login, shared galleries, automatic imports, unified profiles and bundled billing are possible future work, not existing features. Stop promotional reminders when the customer opts out. Do not put personal information or sensitive milestones into tracking URLs. Invitations involving minors' photos need appropriate adult authority and product controls; no direct marketing to children is proposed.

## Revenue, partners and cost discipline

Keep revenue recognition, costs and contribution separate by product and customer type. Jodibana operator software, any JodiUSA membership offer, WeddingOS professional software, consumer event purchases and Memoire digital/print orders are different revenue units. Preserve the existing provisional pricing model until the founder approves changes. Do not insert a portfolio subscription, bundle discount or commission percentage by implication.

Long-term value comes from useful repeat occasions and professional relationships, not forcing consumers into indefinite subscriptions. A completed wedding or a member leaving a dating service can represent success. Measure satisfaction, appropriate repeat use and permissioned referrals alongside revenue.

Operators keep their own disclosed membership/service economics. Planners, venues and photographers retain their professional service fees. A future referral or reseller arrangement must define the attributable product sale, attribution period, refunds, discounts, tax/pass-through treatment, payout timing and contribution floor. Do not promise a share of a partner's whole profit. Internal cross-product introductions do not automatically earn an external commission, and two partners must not be paid twice for the same acquisition without an approved rule.

Maintain three cost views: cash contribution, fully allocated operating contribution and owner-investment recovery. Include founder time in operating estimates when consumed; track the founder's stated sweat-equity investment separately to avoid expensing the same effort twice. Allocate shared hosting by measured use or a disclosed interim driver, support by recorded time, and fulfillment directly to orders. Record shared content costs against the products actually benefiting. Existing cost allocations and owner investment amounts are unchanged by this strategy.

No physical sales hire is proposed yet. Founder-led remote discovery should establish repeat demand and support burden. Consider field coverage only after a geographic partner cluster, repeatable onboarding and retained contribution can support its full cost; use the product-specific triggers already in the financial strategy as hypotheses to validate.

## First 30 days of execution

The schedule starts when assigned owners begin work; publication days depend on readiness and explicit approval. No task is marked complete merely because its scheduled day has passed.

- **Days 1–3:** resolve product/brand mapping and JodiUSA public blockers; confirm Memoire destination; finish current WeddingOS and Jodibana activation checks. Collect owner decisions without changing commercial terms.
- **Days 4–7:** finalize four distinct offer briefs and the three priority campaign packets; verify opt-in audience sources and exact owned social accounts; define activation and attribution events. Prepare Memoire demonstration material only from authorized assets.
- **Days 8–14, after approval:** run small, capped releases on approved channels. Start with WeddingOS's verified useful tools. Run Jodibana operator demos and JodiUSA invitation introductions only after their respective gates pass. Log delivery, qualified engagement and activation; no automatic resend.
- **Days 15–21:** review onboarding friction and interviews, fix observed issues and prepare one relevant optional ecosystem handoff. Test handoffs with synthetic data before customers. Submit revised copy or offers for approval.
- **Days 22–30:** compare channel cohorts, product contribution and support workload. Decide which experiments to continue, revise or stop. Recommend expansion using measured evidence, not elapsed time or registrations alone.

## Measurement and decisions still needed

Track qualified visits, signups and meaningful activation separately for each product. WeddingOS activation is a useful result/first save; Jodibana activation is a functioning operator workflow; JodiUSA activation must reflect a relevant consensual interaction; Memoire activation is a reviewed preview or completed deliverable, depending on verified scope. Track opt-in handoff clicks and attributable destination activation without constructing a shared sensitive customer profile. Portfolio lifetime value is an analytical cohort measure, not permission to join personal records.

All experiment volumes, conversion rates, revenue uplifts and ecosystem lifetime-value gains remain unmeasured. No numerical acquisition forecast is asserted here. A cohort-size target should be chosen only after audience availability, invitation capacity and support staffing are known.

Founder confirmations needed before launch: Memoire's canonical name/domain and deliverables; JodiUSA entity/jurisdiction, support and invitation owner, initial community/geography; exact publisher accounts and eligible audience locations; specific campaign payloads and timing. Pricing, commissions, bundles and partner agreements require separate decisions. The ecosystem theme itself is founder direction and does not need reconfirmation.
