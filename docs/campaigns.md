# 90-day campaign templates
Allocate planned effort 70% to LiqMint Individual, LiqMint Institutional, OraToPlus and WeddingOS; 20% to Atmasphere, SDIS and One-Man Hedge Fund; 10% to discovery. Targets are experiments, not forecasts.

Days 1–30: verify product readiness, customer, website, pricing, support and evidence. Interview target customers. Prepare demos and platform-specific manual packages. Target 150 qualified leads, 20 interviews, 10 demos and five strategic conversations across the portfolio, subject to validation.

Days 31–60: launch only ready products after channel rules checks. Consumer template: problem → verified demonstration → trial CTA → activation event → paid conversion. Institutional template: buyer pain → verified controls → demo → scoped proposal → paid pilot. Investor template: verified traction → investor-fit research → individually approved introduction → meeting → diligence.

Days 61–90: compare qualified conversions by UTM source, retention and costs. Stop low-quality channels. Request authentic reviews without manipulation. Prepare evidence-backed investor updates for LiqMint Institutional and Atmasphere. Never imply investment returns or fabricate customer evidence.

Monday metrics and actual-vs-target review; Tuesday educational drafts; Wednesday interviews and demos; Thursday directory packages and experiments; Friday investor pipeline and next-week priorities. Every public action requires its own exact-payload approval. Broad campaign approval never authorizes unlimited outreach.
