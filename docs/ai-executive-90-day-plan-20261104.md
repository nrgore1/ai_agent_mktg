# AgentVire: 90-day AI executive operating plan

Period: **4 November 2026 through 1 February 2027**, America/New_York.

Approved operating scope: Founder-approved 2026-11-04: verified WeddingOS free tools, free JodiUSA, verified Memoire digital features; OraToPlus qualitative feedback until numeric gates pass; LiqMint Institutional manual evidence-review pilot preparation only; internal products remain private; unclear products remain discovery; provisional costs labelled; no Google Cloud. Existing evidence and action-authorization gates remain.

## Accountability and autonomy

35 plans cover CEO, CFO, CMO, CTO and COO across seven units. Existing company-role logins see their assigned plans; founder oversight spans both separate legal entities.

Each plan has three phases, with one automated preparation activity and one verified outcome milestone per phase: 210 activities, comprising 105 preparation and 105 outcome milestones. They are separate from the existing 282-task execution backlog.

The VPS worker produces at most one evidence pack per role/unit/week and another when its phase changes. It inspects current scoped readiness, cases, finance and Vanguard records. Existing local Mistral workers continue bounded next-action selection. Neither worker signs contracts, sends campaigns, deploys product code, moves money or completes business outcomes.

## First seven days

1. Day 1: produce 35 baseline packs and route existing backlog owners.
2. Days 2-3: CTOs reproduce P0 checks; CFOs identify missing period records; CEOs rank three priorities per unit; COOs verify support/onboarding ownership; CMOs prepare one eligible experiment per ready public unit.
3. Days 4-5: implement and test narrow fixes on separate product backlogs; prepare scoped partner/pilot and financing materials; validate claims and recipients before external action.
4. Days 6-7: review observed progress, source freshness and owner/provider dependencies. Record actual outcomes only and avoid retrying unchanged missing-input blockers.

## Weekly role cadence

| Role | Activity target | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | One unit decision review; rank three priorities | Actual relationship sources, accountable owners, decisions and next actions |
| CFO | One updated financial gap and allocation pack | Source period, supplier/support records, actual versus estimated amounts |
| CMO | One experiment package per eligible unit | Exact copy, verified value, destination, sender, consent and attribution |
| CTO | One bounded acceptance-check cycle | Source revision, environment, reproducible checks, limitations and rollback |
| COO | One delivery/support review | Onboarding, support ownership, real feedback, vendor/recovery evidence |

These are planning activity targets, not guaranteed customer, revenue or financing results. Actual sends, conversions and cash remain unknown until evidenced.
