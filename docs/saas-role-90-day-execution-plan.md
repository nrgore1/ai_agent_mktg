# SaaS role 90 day execution plan

Period: 4 November 2026 through 1 February 2027.

Each of the 15 department roles has a plan in each of seven business units: 105 new plans, 630 scheduled activities and 315 manual phase deliverables. Including executives, there are 140 plans and 840 activities. Each phase has a source-linked preparation pack and a separately reviewed business outcome.

## President workflow

Sign in at https://agentvire.com/?view=business. The **SaaS department execution** panel reports plans, interim ownership, review requests, accepted outcomes and overdue outcomes. Use the company, business-unit and role filters in the workspace.

1. In **User management**, assign named people to departments and units. No additional accounts or grants were created by this release. The President remains interim owner until staff are assigned.
2. One eligible enabled account receives unfinished work automatically. Where several people qualify, select the accountable owner in the department panel and record a reason. Disabling or revoking an owner returns unfinished, unlocked work to an eligible person or the President.
3. Open **90-day role plans** for acceptance criteria and dates. Open **Human + AI workspace** for the linked manual deliverable. Supply actual evidence and an attestation when completing it.
4. Completion submits the outcome to **Milestones awaiting President review**. Accept verified evidence or return it with the missing requirements. Staff cannot accept their own milestone.
5. Reopen a manual deliverable when its evidence needs revision. This invalidates its accepted outcome and later dependent accepted outcomes; history is retained.

Auditors remain read-only. Their plan identifies the reviewer; the President records supplied findings and performs manual record changes. Use a dedicated auditor account for independent review.

## Backlog and acceptance rules

81 existing unresolved handoffs were routed to departments using their original manual-task IDs. Executive source accountability remains. Original readiness states, evidence, commercial decisions, cases, accounts and grants were preserved. Completed and protected manual work was not reopened. Unmapped products remain in President discovery.

Phase one verifies a baseline deliverable and preparation pack; it can identify unresolved launch gaps. Later-phase acceptance also requires prior outcomes, current preparation, completed manual evidence and all linked source gates to pass or be explicitly not applicable. Missing sources block acceptance. Completing a handoff never completes its source execution task automatically.

JodiUSA stays free; unsupported OraToPlus numeric grades remain held; LiqMint Institutional financial execution remains excluded. No Google Cloud is used. Billing/invoice workflows do not execute provider actions.

## Role plans

### USER_ADMIN

Measure: Named ownership coverage; access-review exceptions; offboarding evidence.

**Days 1-30: Establish named owners and least-privilege access.** Review assigned-unit users, role needs and first-login handover; record the responsible person for each role without credentials.

**Days 31-60: Verify joiner mover and leaver controls.** Exercise approved account changes and session revocation using synthetic accounts; review real access exceptions with the President.

**Days 61-90: Complete the quarterly access review.** Reconcile enabled access to current responsibilities; document approved removals, protected accounts and unresolved ownership.

### BILLING

Measure: Unreconciled billing cases; tested recovery paths; genuine recovery outcomes.

**Days 1-30: Baseline subscriptions and collection gates.** Inventory applicable free and paid access, renewal and refund states with provider references. Preserve free JodiUSA and disabled live collection where required.

**Days 31-60: Verify billing recovery and customer remedies.** Collect failed-payment, payment-method update, renewal, cancellation and delayed-refund evidence; distinguish tests from live transactions.

**Days 61-90: Review billing quality and leakage.** Reconcile actual billing exceptions and recovery outcomes; report unsupported charges or missing evidence. No charge or refund is executed by this plan.

### INVOICING

Measure: Draft validation exceptions; actual receivables aging; unmatched settlements.

**Days 1-30: Define invoice controls and source records.** Identify the legal issuer, customer authority, currency, line-item sources, numbering and tax-review owner; create a protected sample draft reference.

**Days 31-60: Validate invoice credit and settlement evidence.** Trace eligible draft-to-issue, credit and payment records; use labelled synthetic examples where no genuine invoice exists. Route issue requests for approval.

**Days 61-90: Review receivables and invoice accuracy.** Report actual aging, credits and unmatched settlement, or explicitly report no issued invoices; reconcile to the revenue owner.

### REVENUE

Measure: Source coverage; unexplained differences; unresolved allocation assumptions.

**Days 1-30: Tie out available expense and payment evidence.** Inventory periods and source totals; separate cash, recognized revenue, equipment and owner investment. Identify missing records and provisional allocations.

**Days 31-60: Reconcile one observed operating period.** Match processor, bank and source records; explain deferred revenue, refunds, fees and allocation differences for CFO review.

**Days 61-90: Prepare the quarter reconciliation handoff.** Produce company and unit controls, outstanding exceptions and source-linked economics. Do not describe provisional books as finalized.

### SALES

Measure: Permissioned qualified opportunities; observed activation; actual conversion.

**Days 1-30: Build a qualified discovery queue.** Define buyer fit, genuine source, customer problem, permission status and next owner. Do not convert researched contacts into an opted-in list.

**Days 31-60: Conduct eligible discovery and track first value.** Record actual authorized conversations, objections, trial activation and next steps, or no activity; use only verified offers and destinations.

**Days 61-90: Review opportunity conversion and sales learning.** Compare genuine qualified opportunities, activation and conversion; recommend the next sales experiment with evidence and no invented pipeline.

### PARTNERSHIPS

Measure: Verified partner fit; scoped pilots; measured partner value.

**Days 1-30: Prioritize evidence-based partner pilots.** Research partner identity, mutual value, operating model and conflicts; prepare scoped pilot hypotheses without promising commissions.

**Days 31-60: Prepare and validate a scoped pilot handoff.** Document genuine partner feedback, responsibilities, customer permission and measurable success; route exact terms for approval before commitment.

**Days 61-90: Review partner value and economics.** Compare observed pilot results, service obligations and supported economics; recommend continue, revise or stop with unresolved risks.

### CUSTOMER_SUCCESS

Measure: Observed first value; repeat use; unresolved onboarding objections.

**Days 1-30: Define first-value journeys and support ownership.** Map verified free or eligible product journeys, onboarding steps, consent, escalation and first-value evidence; retain product limitations.

**Days 31-60: Validate onboarding with authorized participants.** Record observed activation, objections, support needs and permitted follow-up. Synthetic tests must not count as customer adoption.

**Days 61-90: Review repeat use retention and service outcomes.** Report actual cohorts and repeat value where data exists, with sources and coverage; propose a measured retention improvement.

### SUPPORT

Measure: Observed response and resolution times; recurring unresolved issues.

**Days 1-30: Establish triage and escalation runbooks.** Define support route, identity verification, severity, responsible owner and safe escalation; avoid unnecessary customer data in evidence.

**Days 31-60: Verify resolution and recovery handoffs.** Exercise representative incidents and authorized requests; link separate product fixes and distinguish synthetic drills from real resolution.

**Days 61-90: Review recurring issues and response quality.** Summarize observed response and resolution times, repeat issues and knowledge gaps; assign prevention work with acceptance criteria.

### PRODUCT

Measure: Evidence-backed acceptance coverage; observed value; unresolved hypotheses.

**Days 1-30: Translate customer needs into acceptance scope.** Review current identity, claims, offer assumptions and constraints; prioritize customer problems with measurable acceptance and an executive reviewer.

**Days 31-60: Validate the highest-value supported journeys.** Collect user evidence and product-backlog acceptance results; retain grading, billing and privacy restrictions until their gates pass.

**Days 61-90: Choose the next quarter from observed value.** Compare observed adoption, quality, service cost and feedback; present a supported roadmap with explicitly labelled hypotheses.

### ENGINEERING

Measure: Reproduced failures; independently verified fixes; repeat failures.

**Days 1-30: Prepare the highest-risk reproducible fixes.** Reproduce assigned failures in authorized environments; link code, diagnosis, tests and rollback on separate product backlogs. Use no Google Cloud.

**Days 31-60: Verify integration and failure handling.** Attach end-to-end, retry, idempotency and recovery evidence for relevant providers and local inference; no unsupported numeric grading.

**Days 61-90: Reduce repeat failure and maintenance risk.** Review regression and operational evidence; document technical debt, mitigations and tested release procedures without claiming unperformed deployments.

### QA

Measure: Applicable gates passed; failures and untested coverage; candidate traceability.

**Days 1-30: Baseline acceptance and denied-access coverage.** Map current version and environment to signup, activation, save/return, roles, limits and recovery checks; retain untested and failed cases.

**Days 31-60: Run scoped end-to-end and recovery checks.** Verify applicable payment/provider flows and failure paths using authorized test data; bind results to the candidate and independently record failures.

**Days 61-90: Review sustained regression and release gates.** Compare current acceptance evidence and remaining gaps; submit release-readiness recommendations only for gates that actually pass.

### SECURITY

Measure: Verified controls; open risk age; remediation evidence.

**Days 1-30: Review access risks and exposed data paths.** Inventory scoped assets, privileged access, anonymous data denial and secret custody; document risks without copying secrets into cases.

**Days 31-60: Verify authorized controls and incident handling.** Review tenant isolation, revocation, rate limits and incident exercises within authorized scope; link remediation evidence and remaining weaknesses.

**Days 61-90: Review residual risks and security ownership.** Record verified fixes, unresolved risk owners and recovery readiness; propose further assessment without claiming independent certification.

### PRIVACY

Measure: Verified consent/rights coverage; unresolved provider and retention exceptions.

**Days 1-30: Inventory consent retention and provider obligations.** Map explicit consent, withdrawal, exports, deletion, media, provider history and backup retention; identify authority and missing provider capability.

**Days 31-60: Verify rights-request and deletion continuity.** Trace authorized product/provider results and restored-backup suppression using synthetic evidence where appropriate; unavailable erasure remains unresolved.

**Days 61-90: Review retention exceptions and audit evidence.** Verify scheduled retention, minimal suppression and unresolved provider obligations; record genuine receipts rather than treating policy as execution.

### SRE

Measure: Observed health; restore and alert coverage; unresolved recovery gaps.

**Days 1-30: Baseline service health backup and recovery ownership.** Inventory relevant VPS services, monitoring, alert owner, external-drive custody and separate recovery-key location; never place secrets in evidence.

**Days 31-60: Verify recovery and operational failure handling.** Perform authorized restore and alert checks, attach checksums and coverage, and retain missing historical recovery evidence; no Google Cloud.

**Days 61-90: Review reliability capacity and recovery results.** Report observed incidents, capacity and restore outcomes; prioritize recurring controls with owners and actual test evidence.

### AUDITOR

Measure: Reviewed sample coverage; supported findings; unresolved exceptions.

**Days 1-30: Define independent evidence sampling.** Review assigned-unit access, cases, financial controls and approval samples; record findings through the President without changing source evidence.

**Days 31-60: Review segregation and exception handling.** Inspect samples of authorization, denied access, reconciliation and recovery; identify missing evidence and retain independent read-only access.

**Days 61-90: Deliver the quarterly assurance findings.** Present supported findings, coverage limits and unresolved exceptions. The President records the signed review; no assurance certification is inferred.
