# AgentVire CEO user guide

## Run both companies through accountable human and AI work

This handbook explains how the President and the human CEO, CFO, CMO, CTO and COO use AgentVire to improve customer acquisition, partnerships, financing and operations. Read the common workflow first, then your role chapter and the relevant business-unit playbook.

AgentVire is the shared operating workspace for Atmakosh LLC and Vireoka LLC. It connects scoped work queues, preparation recommendations, 90-day plans, evidence, human tasks and approval records. The human remains accountable for verified work and customer outcomes.

**Start here:** https://agentvire.com/?view=business

## Your first working session

1. Sign in with your own account. If prompted, change the temporary password, then sign in again. Use a unique password of at least 14 characters.
2. Check the company, business unit and next responsible role filters. A missing view may reflect your role permissions.
3. Open **Human + AI workspace** and read **Assigned work and next actions**. Choose one applicable P0 task whose prerequisites can be satisfied.
4. Open the linked case in **Work queues**. Read its current step, acceptance evidence, due date and latest observations before acting.
5. Review the corresponding recommendation in **AI decisions and feedback**. Approve eligible internal preparation, or reject it with a specific reason.
6. Start the assigned manual task, do the work, and save evidence. Update the case and plan separately when their own acceptance criteria are met.
7. End the session with one recorded result, one next owner and one due date. Record a genuine blocker instead of marking unfinished work complete.

## How to read this guide

The President chapter covers portfolio authority. Each executive chapter covers purpose, screen use, routines, AI collaboration, the 90-day outcomes, measures and escalation. Product playbooks explain what “first meaningful use” means for each unit. The final pages provide reusable records, measurement rules and manual-action priorities.

This role edition includes the shared workflow, your role chapter, product playbooks and handoff references. [Open the complete handbook](complete-guide.md) for the other role chapters.

# Use the right record for the right outcome

## Four records that must not be confused

| Record | What completion means | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI recommendation or evidence pack | A human accepts that exact internal preparation artifact | The business task or external action was performed |
| Manual task | The assigned human performed the stated task and recorded evidence | Its linked readiness item automatically passed |
| Business case step | That step’s acceptance evidence is satisfied and any required handoff is accepted | A contract was signed, a card charged or software deployed |
| 90-day outcome activity | The milestone evidence has been reviewed and completed by the President | Every underlying product or commercial gate is waived |

The launch-readiness backlog is another linked source of truth. A passed identity check does not imply that pricing, signup, consent, recovery or claims are ready. Preparation packets may be completed automatically; measured business outcomes cannot.

## Create and work a case

In **Business command center → Work queues**, use **Start a business process**. Select the unit and an available process, enter a clear case title, an enabled scoped owner, next due date and source reference. Add a tenant reference only when useful; it does not grant tenant access. Select **Create case**.

Choose a concrete title such as “WeddingOS planner discovery for a saved-plan pilot,” not “Grow the company.” Your available processes depend on the role that owns their first step.

Open the case and inspect **Next action**. Enter an evidence reference and **Outcome and limitations**. Choose **In progress**, **Blocked** or the appropriate completed/submission option. Tick the verification box only after checking the acceptance criteria. Select **Save evidence**.

When a step changes role, the next responsible human must accept ownership operationally. Use **Update owner and due date** with an enabled, appropriately scoped account. Do not assume that a case automatically acquires a named human at every subsequent handoff.

## Keep work moving without inventing completion

“Pending” means work has not been started; unmet prerequisites may make it effectively blocked. “In progress” means work has begun, not that the customer outcome happened. “Awaiting review” is a submitted handoff. A closed case can be closed without success; preserve the reason.

For every blocker, state the missing fact or dependency, who can resolve it, the next action and its review date. An assumption can support a reversible internal experiment when clearly labelled. It cannot manufacture consent, receipts, legal authority, a passing test or a delivered customer outcome.




# Review AI work and record human execution

## Review a recommendation

1. Open **Human + AI workspace → AI decisions and feedback**. Choose **AI recommendations** or **90-day evidence packs**.
2. Read the action and source references. Open the corresponding **Work queues**, **AI work results** or **90-day role plans** evidence.
3. Check company/unit/role scope, current case version, evidence freshness, prerequisites and whether the action is useful at this stage.
4. Choose **Approve internal preparation** only when those checks pass. Otherwise choose **Reject / return for revision**, explaining the failed condition.
5. For a current, unlocked recommendation, select **Revision focus**, add context and choose **Request AI revision**. Refresh after the worker runs and review the new result separately.

Rejecting a recommendation records a decision; it does not itself request another model run. President-locked decisions must be released by the President before a human role can change them. Older evidence cannot be approved as current.

## Understand the AI boundary

The local worker selects among reviewed next-action plans using scoped facts. It is not unrestricted business chat or an autonomous negotiator. Only the selected focus and fixed evidence flags reach that model; free-text revision context stays in the scoped human record. Writing “send this email” there does not send it.

Weekly 90-day packets summarize evidence and blockers. Neither kind of preparation sends outreach, alters prices, transfers money, deploys software, approves a handoff or completes a case. Use the actual model/status shown in **AI work results**; a model name is not a quality certificate.

## Complete manual and plan work

In **Create a manual task**, select the pairing and human assignee, then specify instructions, acceptance evidence and a source/case reference. Mark a consequential handoff when applicable. It requires exact President authorization before its start or completion can be recorded, plus the external system’s controls.

In **Manual work queue**, select **Start manual work**, perform the work, then **Record completed manual work** with its source, outcome and verified-evidence checkbox. Do not complete a preparation handoff as though it were the underlying release test.

In **90-day role plans**, record milestone evidence and use **ready_for_review** when the full outcome is supported. The President can mark it completed after review. Keep future-phase tasks pending until their planned phase; the existing program cannot be silently reset to a new start date.




# CEO guide for priorities customers and partnerships

## Your business outcome and workspace

Turn opportunities into validated customer problems, qualified pilots and disciplined resource choices. Use **Work queues**, **Vanguard opportunities**, **90-day role plans**, **AI work results** and **Human + AI workspace** within your assigned company/units.

Your CEO login does not include the CFO’s detailed Finance evidence view or other roles’ private case notes. Request a scoped financial conclusion through the workflow; have the President handle cross-role decisions that require unrestricted evidence.

## Daily routine

Review the current role packet and the three most important customer, partner or financing cases. Check that each has a real source, a next decision, an owner and a due date. Resolve your own discovery or qualification step before opening more cases.

Use Vanguard as a source mirror, not a proof of interest. Its inbound synchronization is scheduled every five minutes. A stale or unavailable snapshot is not current relationship evidence. Company-level relationships need an explicit unit selected by an authorized portfolio user.

## Qualify a partner

Open or create **Partner discovery to pilot review**. Record the partner’s identity, genuine relationship source, customer problem and mutual value. Distinguish public research, an introduction, a conversation and an agreed pilot.

Complete your qualification only when the relevant acceptance evidence exists. The COO then defines owners, deliverables, dates and success/exit criteria; the CFO validates economics; you prepare the exact commitment for President review. A positive conversation is not a contract.

## Use the CEO AI productively

Choose a focus on ranking source-supported opportunities, preparing a scoped pilot/diligence outline, or resolving missing authority and entity attribution. Ask the human reviewer to challenge whether the next action advances the current case step.

A useful internal instruction is: “Compare these three evidenced opportunities by customer need, time to learning, blocked dependencies and delivery capacity. State what remains unknown.” Perform any deeper analysis in an authorized working document; the current bounded selector does not execute free-text analytical instructions.

## Weekly routine

Review one customer learning, one partner dependency and one resource tradeoff per unit. Stop work with no validated problem or attainable next test. Hand the CMO a supported problem and buyer hypothesis, the COO a concrete pilot scope, and the CFO exact commercial assumptions.




# CEO guide for outcomes and financing readiness

## Your 90 day acceptance path

| Period | Outcome to prepare and evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Confirm accountable owners, ranked opportunities and a scoped financing objective |
| Days 31–60 | Review actual discovery/pilot evidence; record continue, revise or stop and remaining diligence gaps |
| Days 61–90 | Select next-quarter priorities using observed results, constraints and explicit resource decisions |

Use **Record milestone evidence** for the outcome, not merely a list of meetings. A phase can be successfully reviewed with a justified stop decision; it does not require an invented customer win.

## Measure decision quality and progress

Track qualified opportunities with a verified problem and buyer, pilots with both parties’ owners and agreed criteria, time from discovery to a decision, and the number of material assumptions resolved. Report the denominator and reporting period.

Track financing as stages: evidence preparation, appropriate introduction, actual meeting, diligence request and documented commitment. Only supported events advance a stage. Pipeline value and an expression of interest are not cash or committed capital.

## Vireoka financing example

The owner’s objective is a $3 million Vireoka raise covering BankTech/Rumika and LiqMint Individual/Institutional projections, excluding TCompprop. Preserve that scope in the financing case.

The CFO must distinguish projections from actual revenue, reconcile monthly assumptions and provide current cash, liabilities, ownership and use-of-funds evidence. The CEO verifies the actual introduction and prepares exact recipient-specific materials. Appropriate professional review and the existing external-action gates remain prerequisites. Do not promise compensation for investment introductions as an ordinary software referral.

## Atmakosh customer and partner example

For WeddingOS, validate whether a planner has a real saved-plan or coordination problem. For the wider life-events ecosystem, test whether customers independently want a next product. Dating-to-wedding-to-memoir is a strategic theme, not proof that any particular individual wants cross-product marketing.

## Escalate when

Escalate a disputed legal entity, unverified financial promise, exclusivity request, unsupported integration commitment, investment-finder compensation or a resource need outside delegated authority. Provide the President a recommendation with alternatives and evidence, not just “please approve.”

**Completion record example:** “Discovery confirmed repeated planning handoffs with the decision-maker; source is the permissioned interview note. Proposed next step is one synthetic-plan evaluation. No price, booking, referral agreement or customer data import was promised.”

Add paid/physical commitments only when delivery, economics and exact authorization support them. This sequence is a recommended operating approach, not evidence of completed customer acquisition.




# Coordinate handoffs without losing accountability

## Follow the implemented process

| Process | Role sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | CMO qualification → CEO needs → CFO economics → CEO reviewed offer → COO customer outcome |
| Campaign | CMO audience → CMO exact package → CFO cost/attribution → CEO reviewed handoff → CMO results |
| Partnership | CEO qualification → COO scope → CFO economics → CEO reviewed commitment → COO pilot results |
| Financing | CEO objective → CFO evidence → CEO relationship → CEO reviewed materials → CEO diligence outcome |
| Onboarding | COO scope → CTO readiness → COO first use → CMO permitted follow-up → COO outcome |
| Support | COO triage → CTO remedy → COO reviewed remedy → COO resolution → CTO prevention |

The “reviewed” consequential handoffs require the President’s decision in the case workflow. They do not sign agreements or execute the external action. Consult **Process library** for the current acceptance wording and additional vendor/product processes.

## Prepare a handoff the next role can use

Include the exact unit, case, customer/tenant reference, completed criterion, evidence date, limitations, next decision, next responsible account and due date. Share a scoped summary appropriate to the receiving role, not an unrestricted copy of private source records.

If the next role lacks access, ask the President to resolve a legitimate assignment or route a suitable summary. Do not solve this by sharing passwords or moving confidential documents into public links.

## Reusable evidence note

**Objective:** the exact result this step requires.

**Observed result:** what happened, including counts and dates.

**Source:** authorized document, case, test or provider reference.

**Scope:** company, unit, tenant, environment and reporting period.

**Limitations:** what was not tested, confirmed or delivered.

**Gate decision:** pass, fail or not yet evidenced, with the criterion.

**Next owner and date:** the person/account and next check.

## Reusable President decision request

State the exact proposed action and legal entity; explain why now; show the alternatives, expected benefit, cost/exposure and evidence; list unmet gates; define rollback/stop conditions; and identify the accountable executor.

For a campaign, attach the exact sender, recipient eligibility, copy and destination. For a production change, attach the exact change, tests and recovery plan. For finance/partner commitments, attach the exact scope and reviewed economics. A generic “approve marketing” request does not replace these records.




# Run a weekly performance review that changes decisions

## Use one scorecard and consistent definitions

| Dimension | Measure | Evidence owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand | Qualified opportunities with verified problem, buyer and appropriate permission | CMO with CEO |
| Activation | Eligible users reaching the product-specific first value within a defined window | COO with CTO |
| Retention | Activated users returning for meaningful use in the defined cohort/window | COO with CMO |
| Economics | Source-supported net revenue, included costs and separately labelled scenarios | CFO |
| Delivery | Critical defects, failed workflows, incidents and restore/monitoring evidence | CTO with COO |
| Execution | Overdue decisions, P0 blocker age and tasks with accountable next actions | CEO and President |

Some measures need manual evidence or product instrumentation. The current business dashboard’s record counts are not automatically a complete revenue, retention or profitability analytics system. State the source, window, denominator and missing coverage for every reported metric.

## Suggested weekly meeting

Before the meeting, each role updates current evidence and its top three next actions. In the meeting, review customer outcomes first, then delivery failures and economics, then decisions and capacity. End with continue/revise/stop for each active experiment, a named owner and a date.

Keep a suggested work-in-progress limit of three priority outcomes per role/unit until the President agrees a different capacity. This is a management recommendation, not a software limit. A blocked task should produce a specific dependency request, not an excuse to start ten unrelated tasks.

## Use the existing calendar

Days 1–30 run 4 November–3 December 2026; days 31–60 run 4 December–2 January; days 61–90 run 3 January–1 February 2027. Each role/unit has preparation and outcome activities for each phase. Review current-phase evidence weekly and preserve the original dates when reporting slippage.

## Diagnose common problems

If a queue looks empty, refresh and check the filters and assigned scope. If an update says the record changed, refresh, reread the latest version and resubmit only the still-valid decision. If an AI result is older, wait for current preparation or use the permitted revision/retry path; do not approve it as current.

A missing tab usually reflects access rules. A blank actual margin reflects incomplete recognized financial evidence. “Ready” AI status means a prepared result is available, not that a launch is ready. Completing a manual handoff does not automatically close the source backlog item.
