# Launch & Learn: portfolio marketing operations

The Launch Desk prepares and manages the six Days 31–60 activities across all 17 products. Initial preparation creates 17 campaign dossiers, 38 channel-specific draft packages, 17 first-value experiments and 102 activity records. Preparation is idempotent and does not send, publish, manufacture leads or alter real outcomes.

## Launch priority campaigns

Allocate 70% of effort to LiqMint Individual, LiqMint Institutional, OraToPlus and WeddingOS; 20% to Atmasphere, SDIS and One-Man Hedge Fund; 10% to discovery. Verify each product's actual availability, destination and exact claims. Public-site observations are useful evidence but do not establish commercial readiness. Unknown products remain in discovery.

Review campaign copy, destination and the publishing account in Launch Desk. Reddit needs an exact subreddit and its current rules. The founder must personally write final Hacker News submissions. Save the reviewed version, then approve its exact hash. Manual packages can only be exported after approval. Export is not submission. Record the real published URL and an explicit founder receipt note to track a founder-reported submission; it is not independent proof of customer acquisition.

## Acquire and activate users

Each product has a proposed first-value journey, activation event and opt-in follow-up sequence. Add actual interested contacts to Pipelines, record explicit product-specific consent and its source, and draft individual next-step emails. A draft binds the sender, recipient, title, body, stream and unsubscribe placeholder. Review and approve before queueing. No bulk email or speculative prospect import occurs.

LiqMint Individual and Institutional use their authorized Postmark credentials with naren@vireoka.com. Other products use the authorized OraToPlus Postmark credential with naren@atmakosh.com. Secrets live only in the protected VPS configuration and application data volume; snapshots and audit records contain no token. Credentials are validated through Postmark's read-only server API. Actual sender-signature acceptance still depends on the Postmark account configuration.

Existing Postmark account usage terms apply. Paid advertising and new purchases remain disabled. Broadcast unsubscribe handling stays with Postmark; local opt-outs also block messages. Opted-out, unconsented, cross-product and multi-recipient actions are blocked. API acceptance is not delivery: receipts report provider acceptance separately. Uncertain network outcomes are quarantined and never automatically resent.

## Demonstrations and feedback

Use each product's qualification questions, scoped demo agenda and pilot proposal checklist. Confirm participants and use synthetic or authorized data. Actual completed demos require notes and a meeting/evidence reference. Feedback records require actual customer observations and a source; interview scripts alone are not feedback. These records do not invent analytics events or customer outcomes.

## Measure and iterate

Emit real events using product-scoped ingestion keys. Include product, campaign and UTM attribution. Instrument landing views, signups, trials, the proposed product-specific activation event, demos, pilot proposals, paid conversions and feedback as appropriate. Ingestion must occur server-side after the actual action succeeds. Never expose ingestion keys in browser JavaScript or record a subscription before payment confirmation.

The manager reviews actual records daily while the service runs; Friday reviews are labeled weekly decision reviews. It recommends instrumentation when data is absent and investigation of message/onboarding fit after 100 actual landing views with zero activations. These are operating heuristics, not validated conversion predictions. No budget, offer or live campaign changes automatically.

Activity completion derives from observed records against explicit planning targets. Sixteen prepared interview scripts are not sixteen completed interviews. Roadmap checkboxes remain founder planning decisions; Launch Desk shows the measured operating progress separately.
