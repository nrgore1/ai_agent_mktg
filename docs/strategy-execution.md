# Strategy execution

Founder/admin desk: `/?view=execution`. Read-only GET `/api/execution`; CSRF-protected founder/admin POST `/api/execution/prepare` and `/api/execution/task`. Source comes from the private economics snapshot, never repository financial data. A missing plan cannot silently fall back to older price assumptions.

Fourteen evidence-linked tasks for each of 17 products, with priority, wave, owner role, effort assumption and dependencies. Three prepared specifications per product are marked preparation completed. Customer outcomes and product launch readiness are never inferred from specifications. Public HTTPS probes prove reachability only.

All pricing, partner and campaign tasks remain awaiting separate founder approval; no commercial completion, pricing mutation, payout or publishing endpoint exists here. Existing campaign exact-payload approvals remain unchanged. No automatic paid ads or contact outreach. The dashboard exposes private economics to founder/admin accounts only. Exported backlog files are confidential.

Changing the strategy source requires explicit resync, retains prior evidence in history and invalidates outdated task completions. Reopening readiness prerequisites invalidates downstream completions. Manual test completion requires a source, detailed outcome and verification attestation. This is founder-attested evidence, not independent audit. A reachable destination is not a security, entitlement, ownership or compliance attestation.

Deployment probes use GET requests only against explicit owned public HTTPS destinations, with TLS verification, public-IP checks, bounded timeouts and redirect validation. Missing URLs remain blocked; product identity holds persist. No signup, payment, campaign or commission is executed by deployment.
